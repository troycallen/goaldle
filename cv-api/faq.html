Frequently Asked Questions

🤖 What technology is used?

Goaldle uses custom computer vision models to detect and blur players in goal videos.

📅 What eras are covered?

Goals from 2000 onwards (before this it's difficult to find quality footage).

💡 Can I make a suggestion?

Yes! Head to the contact page if you have any feedback or suggestions.
